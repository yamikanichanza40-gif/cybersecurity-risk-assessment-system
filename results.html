Project Results

Implementation outcomes and functional testing results of the Cybersecurity Risk Assessment System.

6. Implementation Results

The Cybersecurity Risk Assessment System was successfully developed using Python, Flask and SQLite. The system provides a centralized platform for managing organizational assets, identifying threats, assessing vulnerabilities and evaluating cybersecurity risks.

The implemented system integrates the major stages of the cybersecurity risk assessment process. It allows users to record relevant security information, calculate risk scores and maintain a risk register for monitoring identified risks.

Implemented System Modules

Asset Management

Supports the registration, viewing, editing and deletion of organizational assets.

Threat Identification

Enables users to record, view, update and delete potential cybersecurity threats.

Vulnerability Assessment

Provides functionality for recording and managing vulnerabilities associated with system assets.

Risk Assessment

Evaluates identified risks using likelihood and impact values to calculate risk scores.

Risk Register

Maintains a centralized record of assessed risks and their corresponding risk levels.

Risk Dashboard

Displays dynamic information about recorded assets, threats, vulnerabilities and assessed risks.

Risk Classification Results

The system calculates risk scores by multiplying the likelihood of a threat occurring by its potential impact. The resulting score determines the risk classification.

Risk Score Risk Level Interpretation
1 – 4 Low Relatively low level of risk.
5 – 9 Medium Requires monitoring and appropriate controls.
10 – 16 High Requires attention and risk reduction measures.
17 – 25 Critical Requires urgent risk treatment.

Conclusion

The development results demonstrate that the Cybersecurity Risk Assessment System provides the core functionality required to support the management and assessment of cybersecurity risks. The implemented modules allow users to manage security-related information, evaluate risks and view assessment records through a centralized dashboard.

The project provides a foundation for improving the organization and monitoring of cybersecurity risk assessment activities.
