PROJECT OVERVIEW

About the Project

Cybersecurity threats and vulnerabilities pose significant risks to the confidentiality, integrity and availability of organisational information systems. Identifying and managing these risks is important for protecting digital assets. This project presents a Cybersecurity Risk Assessment System developed to support the identification, evaluation and management of cybersecurity risks through a centralised web-based platform.

SYSTEM CAPABILITIES

Key Features

Asset Management

Register, view, update and manage organisational information assets.

Threat Identification

Identify and record potential threats that may affect organisational assets.

Vulnerability Assessment

Record vulnerabilities and evaluate their severity.

Risk Assessment

Evaluate risks using likelihood and impact scoring.

Risk Register

Maintain a central record of assessed cybersecurity risks.

Risk Dashboard

View risk statistics, risk distribution and recent assessment records.
